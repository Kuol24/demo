Questions

Is Solo Rehab free?

Yes. Right now every lesson, exercise and tool is free to use.

Is my data private?

Your progress is stored in your own browser or device. We don’t collect it.

Does it replace treatment?

No. Solo Rehab is a self-help tool that works best alongside support, counseling or medical care.

What if I slip?

A slip isn’t failure. Restart your counter, tell someone, and keep going. Your lessons stay with you.

Can I stop drinking suddenly?

If you drink heavily, talk to a doctor first. Stopping suddenly can be dangerous.

Need help right now? If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number. To find a helpline in your country, visit findahelpline.com.
